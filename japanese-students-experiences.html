International Student Experience

Study Abroad at IIT Hyderabad

Discover how Japanese students experience study, research, and campus life at IIT Hyderabad, one of India’s leading technology universities.

Japanese students visit IIT Hyderabad (IITH) through various programs organized mainly by their home universities. These programs offer opportunities for study abroad in India, research collaboration, and international project-based learning at IIT Hyderabad.

These programs generally fall into two broad categories.

Programs at a Glance

✈
Short-term Programs
  • Summer or winter programs
  • Short thematic programs
  • Often organized as extracurricular activities by home universities
🎓
Academic Programs
  • Project-Based Learning (PBL)
  • Short-term laboratory research
  • Semester exchange programs
🌏

Beyond the Classroom

During their stay at IIT Hyderabad, Japanese students engage in a wide range of academic and cultural experiences. These include participating in cutting-edge research, collaborating with IITH students in project-based learning, exploring India’s innovation ecosystem, experiencing campus life, and visiting startups and technology hubs.
